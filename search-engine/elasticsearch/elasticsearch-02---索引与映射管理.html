分词器（Analyzer）

内置分词器

名称行为例子: “I’m eating apples”
standard（默认）按标点分词、小写[i’m, eating, apples]
simple按非字母分词[i, m, eating, apples]
whitespace按空格分词[I’m, eating, apples]
keyword不分词[I’m eating apples]
stop去除停用词[eating, apples]
english词干提取[eat, appl]
